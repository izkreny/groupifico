Icon sizes and spacing

Every place the app puts icons next to each other or next to text, drawn with its own Heroicons (filled since AC 73: solid at 24px, mini at 20px, micro at 16px; a slider size in between takes the nearest set), colours and fonts. Each example shows Today, then the same thing with sliders. Spacing is measured glyph to glyph, the gap you see, whatever invisible button box sits around an icon; the sliders work in those terms and the mock works out the CSS.
